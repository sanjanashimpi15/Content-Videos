[P] Now you will have 2 open ends (open terminals) of 1–strand wires. When you do not touch the open ends anywhere, LED does not glow. It is because it is an open circuit and electricity doesn’t flow through the air. But when you touch the open ends to each other, the LED will glow because it becomes a close circuit & electricity flow through it.
[P] 
[P] 
[P] 
[P] 
[P] Now place the open ends on piece of paper. The LED doesn’t glow since paper is a bad conductor (insulator). When you place the open ends on any metal, the LED glows since metals are good conductor and allows current to flow through them.
[P]

[P] Information: 
[P] 
[P] 
[P] An open circuit is a circuit in which one of the jump wires is not connected to any part in the circuit. Also, if a wire is not connected to the positive or negative pole of the Battery in a circuit, then the circuit will not work.

[P] 
[P] 
[P] 
[P] In this experiment pencil lead acts like a fuse, allowing current to pass through it. When it is broken, it becomes open circuit and hence no current passes further.
[P] 
[P] 
[P] 
[P] Information: 
[P] 
[P] 
[P] Sometimes the voltage or current supplied by the power source increases due to some fluctuation (a kind of fault); because of this the power consumed by appliances increases which can cause damage to it (sometimes even Fire). The ‘Fuse’ is an electrical part that is connected before any output i.e. in the initial path of the circuit to protect the components from damage. 
[P] 
[P] 
[P] 
[P] 
[P] There is a filament of conducting material used inside a fuse. It melts whenever current increases. When it melts it breaks the connection (making an open circuit) and hence no electricity passes further. The material used for filament in a fuse is zinc, copper, silver, 
[P] aluminium
[P]  or alloys.
