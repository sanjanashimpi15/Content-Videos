## B. Narration (about 140 words per minute, pauses left between scenes)

**S01 (0:00)** Let's learn how to insert the battery into the Battery ePod.

**S02 (0:06)** You need two things: the Battery ePod, and a CR2032 coin cell, a three-volt lithium battery.

**S03 (0:15)** The ePod has a round battery holder in the middle, with a connector on each side.

**S04 (0:22)** This is the coin cell. The flat side with the writing is the positive side, marked plus.

**S05 (0:29)** Pick up the ePod and hold it flat, with the battery holder facing up.

**S06 (0:35)** Take the battery in your other hand and bring it to the holder.

**S07 (0:41)** Keep the positive side facing up. Lower the battery into the holder and press gently.

**S08 (0:48)** Press until the battery sits flat and level in the holder. It should not wobble.

**S09 (0:56)** Tilt the ePod slightly to check the battery is fully seated, with no gaps at the edge.

**S10 (1:04)** To recap: position it, lower it in, press flat, and check. Always keep the positive side up.

**S11 (1:14)** To remove it, gently ease the battery out with your fingertip.

**S12 (1:20)** With the battery out, the holder is empty and ready for the next one.
