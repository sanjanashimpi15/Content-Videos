Warnings &amp; Precautions
This kit is intended to be used by children who have attained enough maturity to read, understand and follow all the safety measures.
Human safety – general
Keep away from small children; especially below 7 yrs. age.
The pins available for connection on various parts are pointed and may hurt you if misused. So, handle the ePods with care while using them for experiments.
Never put any part of the kit in mouth or swallow.
Do not touch the fan when it’s moving.
Do not swing or throw any part of the kit on friends, animals &amp; any living being.
Do not put any part in the fire.
Human safety – Battery &amp; power supply
Do not insert any part of the kit in the electrical supply sockets available at your house/institution/class/lab; basically nowhere.
Never connect any other type of Battery or any power source which is not provided with the kit.
Never connect the two Battery terminals (positive &amp; negative) directly to each other unless given in any experiment. Even so, do not keep them connected for long.
Do not attempt to recharge the Batteries provided.
Do not throw Batteries in the fire.
Do not attempt to break or open the Batteries.
Parental guide
Make sure the child follows all the rules and safety measures before beginning with experiments. Kindly assist the child, if he/she is younger than 10 yrs. age, for all experiments, especially the ones containing moving parts.
Kit &amp; parts safety
Use every ePod carefully because misuse can break them.
Always make the connections carefully to avoid damage to the components.
Crosscheck the circuit connections with the circuit diagram for each experiment.
Do not put any part in water without discussing it with your Instructor/Teacher.
Keep all the parts in the box safely so that they do not get lost or broken.
About the ePods
Each of the electronic components which you are going to use has an independent assembly called ‘ePod’. They are made for easier, faster &amp; multiple connections. Whenever you are asked to use a certain component in the experiment (e.g. Motor), it means that you will have to use that component’s ‘ePod’ (e.g. Motor ePod).
ePod Terminals
All the ePods have minimum of 4 connector points known as Nodes for connections, given at the border of the ePod.
Every ePod has at least 2 Nodes. Nodes are named as letters such as P, N, A, B, C, D, and E. Each Node has 4 connector pins on top side.
You can use any or all the pins, as per the circuit.
Connecting the ePods
Different ePods can be connected to each other using the jump wires provided. Hence it becomes very easy to make the connections.
Insert/plug one end of the jump wire to the pin of any one Node of an ePod which needs to be connected as shown.
Use the other end of the jump wire to connect the second component, through any pin of the ePod as suggested in circuit diagram.
Inserting 3V Battery into ePod
We are using 3V (3 volts) Battery in this kit. It is not inserted inside the Battery ePod. We are using a 3V Battery which is commonly known as Button cell or coin Battery.
3V Battery is kept in a sealed pack in one of the sections of the main container box. You will have to remove it from the packing; which can be a difficult job sometimes. The ePod consists of Battery holder which locks the Battery within itself.
Insert the Battery as shown in the figure below.
The printed side of the Battery (or shiny surface) should be on top of Battery ePod.
After inserting Battery you are ready to do connect it with other components as given in experiment worksheets. After performing any experiment, disconnect the wires from the Battery ePod but keep the Battery in the ePod. Don’t worry it will not drain the Battery unless it is connected to any other components.
If a Battery gets drained or you want to remove it, you need to push the lock which you can see on one side of the Battery. You can do it using your fingers or use the screw driver given in your kit. As soon as you push the lock, the Battery will pop a little from the ePod and you can then easily take out the Battery using your hand.
Push this strip towards left (other strip) to remove the 3V Battery from Battery ePod.
Push this strip towards left (other strip) to remove the 3V Battery from Battery ePod.

Symbols &amp; basic information of components
Every experiment contains a Circuit diagram which is a graphical representation of the interconnection between the components. It helps in understanding number of components and wires to be used for connection.
We will use ePods for actual connection, but on paper we use symbols to represent the components within a circuit diagram. Symbols help in understanding number of pins or connection points on a component; it represents the component as well as the ePod.
Following are the advantages of using symbol in circuit diagram –
Symbols are easier &amp; faster to use (draw), irrespective of language.
Symbols are standard and universally accepted, hence easier to understand.
Symbols simplify the circuit diagram and interconnection between components.

In this Kit, the positive terminal is denoted by capital letter P and negative terminal with N.

To check if there is continuity between two points &amp; learn about open circuit.

Now you will have 2 open ends (open terminals) of 1–strand wires. When you do not touch the open ends anywhere, LED does not glow. It is because it is an open circuit and electricity doesn’t flow through the air. But when you touch the open ends to each other, the LED will glow because it becomes a close circuit &amp; electricity flow through it.
Now place the open ends on piece of paper. The LED doesn’t glow since paper is a bad conductor (insulator). When you place the open ends on any metal, the LED glows since metals are good conductor and allows current to flow through them.
Information:
An open circuit is a circuit in which one of the jump wires is not connected to any part in the circuit. Also, if a wire is not connected to the positive or negative pole of the Battery in a circuit, then the circuit will not work.

Insert a pencil lead between the open terminals of these two jump wires (as shown in the diagram).
In this experiment pencil lead acts like a fuse, allowing current to pass through it. When it is broken, it becomes open circuit and hence no current passes further.
Information:
Sometimes the voltage or current supplied by the power source increases due to some fluctuation (a kind of fault); because of this the power consumed by appliances increases which can cause damage to it (sometimes even Fire). The ‘Fuse’ is an electrical part that is connected before any output i.e. in the initial path of the circuit to protect the components from damage.
There is a filament of conducting material used inside a fuse. It melts whenever current increases. When it melts it breaks the connection (making an open circuit) and hence no electricity passes further. The material used for filament in a fuse is zinc, copper, silver, aluminium or alloys.

When you press the switch then LED starts glowing; but if you don’t press then LED doesn’t glow. As long as you press the Tact switch, the LED glows but the moment you release the switch, LED turns OFF.  This is called ‘momentary’ control of output.

Tact switch is a bi-directional component which allows current to flow in both directions, unlike LED and battery which are uni-directional component.

The LED glows initially, even without pressing the switch. This happens because when you press the switch there is a Short circuit through the switch.
Information:
A Logic gate is a circuit which gives a logical output. All logic gates have one or more inputs and one output. They are used to construct logic circuits (e.g. Computer).
Logic 1 is called Logic high = 3 to 12 volts (or 5 volts in digital electronics e.g. Computers); Logic 0 is called Logic low = 0 volt. For all Logic gates, we will make a ‘Truth table’, which is a table that shows all conditions.
When any switch is pressed, we will consider it as ‘Input given &amp; its value will be ‘1’. If switch is not pressed, then it will be considered as ‘Input not given &amp; its value will be ‘0’. When the LED, which is the output in the circuit, glows (output is on) then its value will be considered as ‘1’ and if it does not glow (output is off) then its value will be ‘0’. This circuit acts as a ‘NOT’ logic gate. It is called so, because to get the output we should NOT give an input. NOT gate is also called an ‘Inverter’ since output is opposite of input.

P2. Miner’s helmet (Based on Exp 8)
Problem statement: A team of miners find working in a coal mine difficult due to low light. Design a helmet for them with LED on it to give them more light while working. The switch will be placed inside the cap such that when they wear the cap, switch will be pressed, and LED turns on. When they remove the cap, the switch will be released, and LED will be off automatically.
P3. Doorbell (Based on Exp 11)
Problem statement: Your cousin has built a model of a house using cardboard. He needs your help to incorporate the doorbell facility such that when he presses the switch, the buzzer produces the sound.
P4. Footbell (Based on Exp 11)
Problem statement: You family is redesigning your home, and they need a new type of house bell. Redesign the traditional doorbell into a Footbell, with a switch under the door mat such that when someone stands on it the switch gets pressed and Buzzer turns on.
P5. Portable hand fan (Based on Exp 15)
Problem statement: You have your summer vacation, and you are travelling by train. It’s too hot to travel. Design a portable hand-held fan with Momentary control to help you fight the heat wherever you go.
P6. Electronic rotating dice (Based on Exp 15)
Problem statement: Everyone is ready to play with snakes and ladders, but you can’t find the dice. Create a rotating dice using DC Motor with momentary control such that the DC Motor stops on any random number from 1 to 6. You can play other games using this dice.
Extra materials: Cardboard or paper.
P7. Hypnotizing wheel (Based on Exp 15)
Problem statement: You went to magic show and saw that the magician used a hypnotizing trick. Create your own magic trick with a hypnotizing wheel which has a spiral pattern on it, using DC Motor with Momentary control for rotation.
Extra materials: Paper or cardboard.
P8. Operation theatre light (Based on Exp 19)

When the operation is going on, LED – Red will be on and when you are done with operation LED – Red will be off.
P9. Electronic lantern (Based on Exp 19)
Problem statement: There is a power cut at your home, and you must submit an assignment tomorrow in your school. Design a battery-operated lantern with Latch control that can help you to complete your work.
P10. Model of house (Based on Exp 17 &amp; 20)
Problem statement: Your school teacher has asked everyone to create different types of shelters for humans. Design a model of house which has DC Motor as fan with Latch control, Buzzer as doorbell and LED as tube-light. Light &amp; Fan can be used in toggle mode.

P11. Vacuum cleaner (Based on Exp 22)
Problem statement: You need to clean your room. There are many bits of paper lying on the floor. Create a vacuum cleaner with DC Motor and Slide switch such that when the cleaner is kept close to paper, it gets sucked by motor.
Extra materials: Use plastic bottles to create the structure. Place the DC Motor towards the mouth of the bottle.
P12. Hand mixer (Based on Exp 22)
Problem statement: It is a sunny day, and you have some unexpected guests coming up. Prepare a lemonade maker using DC Motor with latch control to mix the ingredients well.
P13. Cap with fan (Based on Exp 22)
Problem statement: Your school has organized a summer field trip to a village where you will be taught about farming. It’s difficult to work in field during summer due to hot climate. Design a cap for yourself with DC Motor on it. Use a Slide switch to turn it on/off.
P14. Audio–visual alarm (Based on Exp 26)
Problem statement: In a textile factory, there are visually impaired people working with others. They need a safety alarm that can produce sound (audio) alert during an emergency. When everything is normal, LED – Green will be on and during an emergency, LED – Red and Buzzer turns on. This should be controlled by a single Slide switch.
P15. Merry-go-round (Based on Exp 27)
Problem statement: There is an event in your local park and lot of people are going to gather there. You have been given all the equipment to assemble Merry-go-round for the kids. The Merry-go-round will start spinning only when the main switch is turned on. The green light stays on when the Merry-go-round spins. When the switch is turned off, the Merry-go-round stops and red light turns on.
P16. Refrigerator fan and light (Based on Exp 31)
Problem statement: Electronic appliances manufacturer is looking to upgrade the existing refrigerators but adding a unique feature. When user opens the door, switch will be released, LED will glow dim and fan will rotate slower which will stop the cooling process and when user closes the door, switch will get pressed and LED turns off and DC Motor rotates faster which would turn on the cooling process.
P17. Refrigerator door (Based on Exp 33)
Problem statement: A luxury car has a small refrigerator inside it. Sometimes door is left open and all the cold drinks become warm. To prevent this, design a mechanism such that when door is opened, the switch will be unpressed and LED will glow. When door is closed, the switch will be pressed, and LED will turn off.
P18. Patient indicator (Based on Exp 33)

P24. Dynamo with wind energy (Based on Exp 46)
Problem statement: Your friend wants to install dynamo lights in his cycle, but he doesn’t have a dynamo with him.
